Charter on the use of artificial intelligence

The CFVE adopted on June 26 the charter for the informed use of artificial intelligence in pedagogy (IAG) for our establishment.

Publié le 18/09/2025 - Updated on 20/05/2026

AGI burst onto the mainstream at the end of 2022. Since then, it has disrupted and reexamined the relationship between knowledge, learners and teachers.

AGI is both a source of advances, of potential, but also at the source of dangers, of risks.

Destined for teachers and students, the charter affirms UPHF's position in the use of the IAG, sets out the commitments of the establishment, teachers and students, and determines the major principles that must drive any use of this tool.

Discover this charte and feel free to pass it on to your students
